# AI-Augmented content guidelines

## Voice

Write in a practical, clear, optimistic, evidence-oriented, and human-centered voice. Explain what a visitor can understand, decide, or do next.

Avoid excessive AI hype, fear-driven messaging, vague transformation claims, unnecessary jargon, and unsupported performance claims.

## Core principle

Describe AI augmentation as increasing human capability through disciplined partnership with AI while preserving human judgment, agency, and responsibility.

## Terminology

Use [brand-ontology.md](brand-ontology.md) as the terminology source of truth. Keep Lens, Context, Maturity, AAOS, and Learn / Apply / Augment distinct.

## Page writing

- Make the audience and outcome clear near the top.
- Give visitors one primary next action.
- Use secondary buttons for exploration and text links for supporting navigation.
- Connect content to a real workflow, decision, or responsibility.
- Use “Education Leader” in public copy even while the existing route remains `education-administrator`.
- Label Learn assets as free, Apply assets as paid, and Augment services as guided paid support.

## Evidence

Verify numbers, names, testimonials, research references, institutional claims, and product availability before publication. Do not add claims merely to make a page sound authoritative.
