# Corpus Conformance Report

## Scope

The public framework pages were checked against the canonical AAOS corpus files:

- `aaos-definition.adoc`
- `aaos-model-map.adoc`
- `aaos-vocabulary-guide.adoc`

## Conformance results

- AAOS is described as the operating model for reliable, accountable, and scalable AI-assisted work.
- AAOS stages remain **Diagnose, Activate, Controls, Execute, Measure, Scale**.
- AI-Augmented Maturity remains **Aware, Exploring, Experimenting, Integrating, Leading, Augmenting**.
- Maturity is described as a condition or level; AAOS is described as the operating sequence.
- Grounding, validation, workflow integration, accountability, handoffs, and human responsibility remain consistent with the corpus.
- No role-specific maturity or AAOS directories were added.
- Practice pages use illustrative workflows and do not claim customer results.

## Deliberate public-copy choices

- The corpus calls AAOS an **operating model** in its definition. Public pages retain that wording.
- The public architecture uses **Controls** in the stage navigation because that is the approved route label in the migration prompt; the vocabulary guide also uses **Control** for the layer in explanatory prose.
- The public role name **Education Leader** maps to the corpus and legacy **Education Administrator** lens.
- Public pages use concise explanations instead of publishing every corpus model as a separate route.

## Remaining review

The corpus contains a naming conflict between `Activate` and `Amplify` in older material. The public site consistently uses **Activate**, as required by the approved migration prompt and current AAOS route structure.
