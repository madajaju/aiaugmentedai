# AI-Augmented brand ontology

This document is the source of truth for AI-Augmented terminology in site copy, metadata, structured data, and generated content.

## AI-Augmented

AI-Augmented describes the disciplined partnership between people and AI that expands human capability while preserving human judgment, agency, accountability, creativity, and responsibility.

Use the term to describe a capability, practice, person, team, organization, or context. Do not use it to imply that AI replaces human responsibility.

## AI-Augmented Movement

The AI-Augmented Movement is the community and body of practice focused on helping people and organizations build reliable capability with AI.

The movement website is the canonical home for the idea, framework, assessments, progression, resources, and community pathways.

## AI-Augmented Framework

The AI-Augmented Framework is the overall system that connects lenses, maturity, AAOS, and Learn / Apply / Augment support levels.

Use this term when describing the complete model. Do not use it interchangeably with AAOS or maturity.

## AI-Augmented Operating System (AAOS)

AAOS is the repeatable operating method for building and scaling reliable AI-Augmented capability.

The six AAOS stages are:

1. Diagnose
2. Activate
3. Controls
4. Execute
5. Measure
6. Scale

Do not call the Aware through Augmenting progression “AAOS stages.”

## AI-Augmented Maturity

AI-Augmented Maturity describes how deeply AI is integrated into judgment, workflows, teams, or institutions.

The six maturity stages are:

1. Aware
2. Exploring
3. Experimenting
4. Integrating
5. Leading
6. Augmenting

Maturity answers where a visitor or organization is. AAOS explains how capability advances.

## Lens

A lens identifies the visitor’s role and the guidance that fits that role.

Current lenses are Individual, Team Leader, Organization Leader, Student, Teacher, and Education Leader.

A lens answers who the visitor is. A lens is separate from a context, maturity stage, and support level.

## Context

A context identifies the domain in which the framework is applied.

Current context: Education.

Planned or future contexts may include Business, Healthcare, Legal, and Government. Contexts may contain several relevant lenses.

## Learn

Learn is the free level of support. It includes educational material such as articles, podcasts, videos, guides, definitions, and assessments. Learn assets may use the shop’s $0 access flow.

## Apply

Apply is the paid self-service level of support. It includes tools, playbooks, workbooks, templates, decision guides, and other assets that help a person or team put the framework into practice.

## Augment

Augment is the guided level of support. It includes workshops, briefings, assessments, advisory services, speaking, coaching, and facilitated transformation with experts.

## AI-Augmented Education

AI-Augmented Education applies the framework to students, educators, researchers, administrators, technology teams, and institutional leaders.

It builds responsible capability across the education community while respecting the different missions, workflows, risks, and responsibilities of each group.

## AI-Augmented Institute

Use AI-Augmented Institute only if it has been formally established as an organization, stewardship body, standards body, research group, or credentialing entity. Do not present it as an existing institution until that governance and identity are confirmed.

## Hallucination Debt

Hallucination Debt is the hidden operational cost created when unverified AI output enters work and accumulates downstream errors, rework, and loss of trust.

Use the term to explain why verification, review depth, ownership, and reliable workflows matter. Do not use it as a synonym for every AI error.

## Writing rules

- Describe AI augmentation as increasing human capability.
- Preserve human judgment, agency, and accountability in claims and calls to action.
- Keep Lens, Context, Maturity, AAOS, and Learn / Apply / Augment distinct.
- Prefer concrete outcomes and workflows over broad claims about transformation.
- Do not invent adoption numbers, testimonials, customer names, research citations, or performance claims.
