About

Make human capability stronger with AI.

AI-Augmented is a movement for people who want to use AI with more capability and more responsibility. The goal is disciplined partnership: AI can extend what people do while people retain judgment, agency, and ownership.

Understand the movementFind your starting point

Why this movement exists

AI can make work faster, but speed alone does not make work reliable. Fluent output can hide weak context, unsupported assumptions, missing perspectives, or unclear ownership.

AI-Augmented helps people improve the whole practice around AI: the capability to do the work, the controls that make it trustworthy, and the workflow that carries quality through review and handoff.

What we believe

  • Human capability comes first. AI should strengthen expertise, reasoning, creativity, and decision quality.
  • Context matters. Good use starts with the purpose, domain knowledge, constraints, and consequences of the work.
  • Validation must match the stakes. Important work needs visible evidence, named review, and clear accountability.
  • Practice improves through learning. Teams should measure what changes, reduce correction load, and scale only when control stability supports it.

How the framework helps

AI-Augmented Maturity describes the condition of a person, team, or organization as practice develops. AAOS describes how reliable work moves: Diagnose, Activate, Controls, Execute, Measure, and Scale.

They are separate dimensions. The framework turns a broad intention into a practical next step that can be tested in real work.
