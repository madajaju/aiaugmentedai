Augment: design safer clinician-AI partnership

Get guided support to redesign clinical information work while preserving professional interpretation, patient safety, and approved-use boundaries.

What guided work can address

Clinical information flow

Map where AI prepares, summarizes, compares, or surfaces questions and where the clinician must verify and decide.

Safety and review

Set review thresholds for patient-facing summaries, recommendations, medication, triage, and escalation.

Practice improvement

Reduce correction load with approved workflows, source capture, failure logging, and a repeatable Clinical Integrity Packet.

Engagement outputs

  1. Clinical workflow map with approved and prohibited AI roles.
  2. Patient-data and access boundary for the workflow.
  3. Consequence-matched validation ladder and escalation route.
  4. Clinical Integrity Packet template and review checklist.
  5. Pilot evidence covering accuracy, omissions, correction load, and safety exceptions.

Clinical signoff

A qualified clinician remains responsible for interpretation and action. A validator confirms clinical fit and evidence; an approver accepts residual risk before a consequential workflow is released.

Bring to the conversation

Bring a de-identified workflow, sample outputs, current review steps, known failure modes, approved-tool constraints, and the decision you need to make. Do not bring identifiable patient information unless the approved channel explicitly permits it.
