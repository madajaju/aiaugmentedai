Augment: build a safe health AI operating model

Get guided support to connect clinical safety, privacy, security, governance, workforce capability, and measurable operating discipline.

What guided work can address

Governance architecture

Translate safety, privacy, security, procurement, and professional obligations into usable roles, policies, controls, and exceptions.

Workflow integration

Connect clinical, operational, and administrative workflows to validation ownership, Integrity Packets, monitoring, and escalation.

Scale readiness

Use live evidence to decide whether a bounded implementation should expand, change, pause, or stop.

Engagement outputs

  1. Health AI use-case inventory with consequence and data classification.
  2. Approved-use boundary and clinical, privacy, security, and operational control map.
  3. Validation and approval matrix with named accountable roles.
  4. Health AI Governance Integrity Packet and monitoring dashboard definition.
  5. Scale decision backed by safety, quality, workforce, equity, and unresolved-risk evidence.

Leadership signoff

Organizational leaders decide whether to release or scale. Qualified clinical, privacy, security, and operational owners validate the evidence; material residual risk is accepted explicitly or the work does not advance.

Bring to the conversation

Bring a bounded use case, data flow, current policy, clinical and operational owners, safety concerns, tool constraints, workforce impact, and the decision horizon. The engagement should leave the organization able to govern the workflow without depending on an outside advisor.
