Augment: build reliable AI-supported care coordination

Get guided support to improve shared context, handoffs, follow-through, and escalation without making responsibility ambiguous.

What guided work can address

Handoff design

Create a transfer pattern that preserves care goals, current context, open questions, next action, owner, and review status.

Cross-role coordination

Clarify what clinicians, staff, patients, and systems each contribute and where qualified judgment must intervene.

Exception handling

Make missing information, conflicting instructions, safety concerns, and failed follow-through visible and actionable.

Engagement outputs

  1. Care coordination workflow and handoff map.
  2. Minimum-necessary data and role-based access rules.
  3. Care Handoff Integrity Packet with outcome, evidence, validation, ownership, and risk.
  4. Review and escalation matrix for routine, urgent, and high-consequence changes.
  5. Pilot report covering handoff return rate, missed context, and unresolved exceptions.

Team signoff

Receiving teams must be able to act safely without reconstructing the sender's intent. The accountable professional validates consequential content and explicitly escalates unresolved risk.

Bring to the conversation

Bring a de-identified handoff, current care-team roles, examples of missed context or rework, system boundaries, and the decision you need to make about the workflow.
