Apply: make care-team handoffs safer

Use AI to organize shared context and follow-through while the accountable professionals retain interpretation, escalation, and care responsibility.

Choose a care-team workflow

Handoff preparation

Carry the care goal, relevant context, open questions, next action, owner, and escalation status into the receiving team.

Care-plan review

Use AI to surface missing context or conflicting instructions, then have the appropriate professional validate what can be acted on.

Follow-through

Track whether the agreed action happened, what changed, and which unresolved issue needs escalation rather than quiet carry-forward.

Safety controls and review thresholds

  • Low consequence: administrative coordination may use light review if patient data and access remain controlled.
  • Medium consequence: care coordination or patient-facing handoffs require a named reviewer, current source context, and clear next ownership.
  • High consequence: changes to treatment, triage, discharge, medication, or escalation require independent professional validation and explicit approval before action.

Practice output

Complete a Care Handoff Integrity Packet with outcome, visible assumptions, evidence and date, validation status, Prepared By, Validated By, Approved By where required, and residual risk.

Approved-use boundaries

Use approved systems, minimum necessary information, role-based access, and defined escalation routes. Do not allow AI to create a silent handoff: unresolved uncertainty must be visible to the next qualified person.
