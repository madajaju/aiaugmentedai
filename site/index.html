The framework

The AI-Augmented Operating System

The AAOS is the six-stage framework behind everything on this site. It is the system visitors explore after they know their lens and current augmentation maturity.

1

Diagnose

Identify the real posture before acting.

2

Activate

Enable the first reliable loops for AI use.

3

Controls

Embed trust and accountability through shared disciplines.

4

Execute

Standardize the integrated loop for AI-assisted work.

5

Measure

Track reliability and control stability across the system.

6

Scale

Expand adoption based on proven evidence of trust.

Built on use, not theory

Drawn from how professionals, educators, and leaders actually adopt AI, not from how they say they will.

Six stages, one path

Diagnose, Activate, Controls, Execute, Measure, Scale. The same framework whether you are an individual, a team, or an organization.

Diagnostic, not aspirational

The AAOS tells you exactly where you stand today, not just where it would be nice to end up.

FAQ

Questions People Ask First

These are the shortest answers to the questions this site is designed to solve.

What does AI-Augmented mean?

It means developing the habits, judgment, and workflows to use AI reliably, responsibly, and effectively.

What is AAOS?

AAOS is the AI-Augmented Operating System, the six-stage framework used throughout this site.

Who is this for?

Individuals, team leaders, organization leaders, students, teachers, and education leaders.

How do I know my stage?

Start with the readiness assessment. It identifies your current augmentation maturity level and gives you the next step.

What should I do first?

Choose your lens, take the assessment, then follow the AAOS path that matches your role.

How is this different from just using AI tools?

Tool use is only the beginning. AI-Augmented means building repeatable capability that improves outcomes over time.

What is an AI-Augmented team?

A team with shared language, reliable workflows, review practices, and clear ownership for human and AI work.

What is AI-Augmented education?

It develops responsible AI capability across students, educators, researchers, administrators, technology teams, and leaders.

How should students use AI responsibly?

Use AI transparently, verify its output, protect sensitive information, and keep ownership of your learning and judgment.

How should teachers adapt to AI?

Set clear expectations, teach verification, and design learning around judgment, evidence, and meaningful practice.

How should universities govern AI?

Use clear ownership, risk-based review, data and privacy controls, transparent expectations, and capability building.

What is Hallucination Debt?

The hidden cost of unverified AI output: downstream errors, rework, and loss of trust.
