Apply AI workflow tools with your team

Turn a shared AI idea into a workflow that people can follow, review, and improve together.

Choose one recurring team task. Make the intended outcome, AI contribution, human decisions, handoffs, and review points visible before you change the way the work is done.

Assess your starting point

Use a team workflow kit

Decision brief

Record the question, context, options, assumptions, owner, and decision required from the team.

Handoff record

Show what AI contributed, what was checked, what remains open, and who owns the next step.

Review rhythm

Set a recurring time to inspect results, discuss failure modes, and update the shared workflow.

Run the first team cycle

  1. Choose: select one bounded workflow with a clear team outcome.
  2. Agree: define the context, tool boundaries, human owner, and review standard.
  3. Run: use AI for the agreed task and record the handoff information.
  4. Reflect: review the result and decide what to keep, change, or stop.

The first cycle should produce learning the team can use. Keep the scope small enough to see whether the new workflow improves the work.
