Help your team work with AI together

Build shared workflows, reliable handoffs, and clear ownership for AI-supported work.

Team leaders create the conditions for consistent practice. That means giving people shared language, agreeing on review points, and making it clear who owns the final decision or deliverable.

Understand where you are

What teams need to practice

Shared context

Give the team a common understanding of the task, inputs, constraints, and definition of good work.

Reliable handoffs

Make assumptions, AI contributions, review status, and next ownership visible as work moves between people.

Learning loops

Use recurring reviews to capture what helped, what failed, and what the team should change next.

Make the workflow easy to follow

  1. Agree on the outcome and the person accountable for it.
  2. Define where AI may assist and what information it may use.
  3. Set the review point before work is handed to the next person.
  4. Discuss the result and update the shared practice.

A team workflow becomes dependable when people can explain how work moves, where judgment belongs, and what evidence supports the result.

Practice shared decisions

Use a team decision brief to align a choice, or explore an operating rhythm for recurring review and improvement.
