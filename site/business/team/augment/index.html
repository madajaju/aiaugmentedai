Get guided support for team adoption

Help your team move from individual experiments to a shared, reliable way of working with AI.

Guided support can help you facilitate alignment, design a team workflow, clarify review and handoff practices, and create the habits that make improvement part of the work.

Start a team conversation

When a team needs guided support

People are working differently

The team needs shared language, expectations, and a common workflow for AI-supported work.

Handoffs are unclear

AI contributions, review status, assumptions, or ownership are getting lost as work moves between people.

Adoption needs momentum

The team needs facilitation and a practical cycle for testing, reviewing, and improving a new practice.

What support can produce

  • A shared description of the workflow and the outcome it should produce.
  • Clear human ownership, review points, handoff expectations, and escalation paths.
  • A bounded team experiment with a way to capture results and failure modes.
  • A repeatable operating rhythm for learning and deciding what to change next.

The aim is to help the team build its own capability. Guided work should leave people with practices they can explain and continue using.

Prepare for the first conversation

Bring one recurring team workflow, the people involved, the handoff or adoption problem you see, and any constraints that matter. A decision brief or operating rhythm can help frame the starting point.
