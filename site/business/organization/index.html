Build an AI-Augmented organization

Connect AI opportunity to strategy, governance, capability, and measurable work.

Organization leaders set direction, define decision rights, protect trust, and create the conditions for teams to adopt useful AI responsibly.

Understand where you are

What changes at organization scale

AI adoption is an operating change, not only a technology decision. Leaders must decide where AI can help, who owns the result, what evidence is required, and how learning moves between teams.

Set direction

Prioritize work where AI can support the mission and define what a useful outcome looks like.

Make decisions visible

Clarify ownership, review depth, escalation paths, and the controls that apply to each workflow.

Build capability

Give people shared practices, feedback, and time to develop reliable AI-supported work.

Use an operating view of readiness

A useful organization dashboard connects four questions:

  • Opportunity: Which workflows or decisions deserve attention first?
  • Ownership: Who is accountable for the human outcome?
  • Controls: What privacy, security, quality, and review practices are required?
  • Evidence: How will leaders know whether the change is helping?

This view helps leaders fund learning and controls together so adoption can grow without losing accountability.

Practice organization-level decisions

Start with a bounded initiative and make the decision record explicit. Use a governance brief to frame ownership and controls, or a team decision brief to connect leadership intent to day-to-day work.

Move from maturity to action

Use the shared maturity model to describe your current pattern. Use AAOS to sequence the next improvement across people, process, technology, and evidence.
