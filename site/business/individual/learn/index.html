Learn the foundations of AI-Augmented work

Build the judgment and vocabulary you need to use AI with confidence and care.

Effective individual use begins with the work itself. Understand the context, give AI the right inputs, check what it produces, and keep ownership of the final result.

Explore the framework

Four ideas to practice

Grounding

Give AI relevant context, source material, constraints, and a clear definition of the task.

Validation

Check facts, reasoning, completeness, and fit against the standards of your work.

Correction

Use errors and weak results as feedback to improve the request, inputs, or workflow.

Ownership

Keep human responsibility for decisions, sensitive information, and the final work product.

Learn through a real task

Choose a recurring task you already understand. Compare your usual process with an AI-assisted version, write down the review points, and decide whether the result is clearer, faster, or more useful.

Small experiments create better learning than tool collecting. Keep the task bounded so you can see what changed and why.
