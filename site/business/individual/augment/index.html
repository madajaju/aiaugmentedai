When guided support is useful

The task matters

You need to improve a recurring workflow where quality, time, or clarity affects important work.

The path is unclear

You have tried AI tools but need help choosing an approach, setting boundaries, or deciding what to verify.

The workflow must last

You want a repeatable practice that you can explain, review, and improve as your work changes.

What to bring to the conversation

  • A recurring task and the outcome you need it to produce.
  • An example of the inputs, constraints, or standards that shape the work.
  • Questions about privacy, verification, tool choice, or where human judgment belongs.

Start with the smallest useful workflow. The goal is a clear next experiment, a review method, and a practical way to learn from the result.
