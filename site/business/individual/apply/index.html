Apply AI to one workflow you own

Turn a useful AI idea into a dependable part of your daily work.

Choose a bounded task such as drafting, research, planning, or analysis. Define what good work requires, decide where your judgment is needed, and improve the workflow through deliberate review.

Find your starting point

A simple way to apply the method

1. Bound the work

Name the task, intended outcome, inputs, constraints, and the person responsible for the result.

2. Add AI assistance

Use AI for a defined part of the workflow, such as generating options, organizing information, or finding gaps.

3. Review and repeat

Check the output against your standards, record what changed, and keep the steps that make the work more reliable.

Keep responsibility visible

AI can help with speed and range, but you remain accountable for the decision and the final work. Protect sensitive information, verify important claims, and make the review step part of the workflow.
