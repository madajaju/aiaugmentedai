AI changes how you gather information, draft work, test ideas, and make decisions. People set direction, interpret context, validate consequential claims, and own the outcome.

Understand where you are

What is changing

AI changes how you gather information, draft work, test ideas, and make decisions. AI can help with preparation, comparison, drafting, and pattern finding while responsibility remains with people.

Three capabilities that matter

Grounded work

Give AI the relevant purpose, source material, constraints, and quality standard before asking it to produce an answer.

Judgment at the point of use

Check important claims, notice weak assumptions, and decide what can be used before a draft becomes work you stand behind.

Reliable personal workflows

Turn one useful pattern into a repeatable cycle with a visible outcome, human review, ownership, and a record of what improved.
