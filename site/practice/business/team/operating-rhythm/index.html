Team Operating Rhythm

A team uses AI to prepare a recurring operating meeting while preserving the decisions, owners, unresolved questions, and follow-up work that the team must act on. This is an illustrative workflow, not a customer case study.

Problem

A team uses AI to prepare a recurring operating meeting while preserving the decisions, owners, unresolved questions, and follow-up work that the team must act on.

Human Role

The team agrees on the meeting outcome and quality standard, reviews the preparation together, resolves disagreements, and assigns the next owners.

Where AI Helps

AI can summarize prior decisions, organize updates, compare current work to commitments, draft an agenda, and surface items that need human discussion.

Where Human Judgment Stays

The team decides priorities, validates status and risks, handles exceptions, and makes the final commitments visible to the people responsible for delivery.

Workflow

  1. Define the outcome and decision boundary.
  2. Gather context and ask AI for a bounded first pass.
  3. Review assumptions, evidence, and missing perspectives.
  4. Revise, document ownership, and measure what improved.

AAOS Mapping

Diagnose where meetings lose context or follow-through; Activate a bounded preparation pattern; establish Controls for sources and access; Execute with the team in review; Measure decision and follow-through quality; Scale only when the rhythm improves the work.

Common Failure Modes

  • Starting with a tool before defining the work.
  • Accepting fluent output without checking assumptions.
  • Leaving ownership or review implicit.

What Good Looks Like

The meeting record acts as an Integrity Packet: decisions, evidence, assumptions, owners, validation questions, and unresolved risks remain visible after the meeting.
