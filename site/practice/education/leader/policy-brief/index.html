Education Policy Brief

An education leader uses AI to organize policy inputs before discussing a change that affects teaching, assessment, privacy, or student support. This is an illustrative workflow, not a customer case study.

Problem

An education leader uses AI to organize policy inputs before discussing a change that affects teaching, assessment, privacy, or student support.

Human Role

The education leader defines the institutional question, includes affected faculty and students, checks the sources, and owns the recommendation and consultation process.

Where AI Helps

AI can organize policy inputs, compare options, draft a plain-language brief, and surface questions about implementation, equity, privacy, and support.

Where Human Judgment Stays

People decide what policy direction is appropriate, validate claims and stakeholder impacts, address exceptions, and approve the next consultation or action.

Workflow

  1. Define the outcome and decision boundary.
  2. Gather context and ask AI for a bounded first pass.
  3. Review assumptions, evidence, and missing perspectives.
  4. Revise, document ownership, and measure what improved.

AAOS Mapping

Diagnose the policy question and affected groups; Activate a bounded research and drafting use; establish Controls for authoritative sources and privacy; Execute with stakeholder review; Measure understanding and implementation impact; Scale only when the practice is trusted.

Common Failure Modes

  • Starting with a tool before defining the work.
  • Accepting fluent output without checking assumptions.
  • Leaving ownership or review implicit.

What Good Looks Like

The policy brief carries an Integrity Packet with its outcome, assumptions, source evidence, human validation, accountable owner, approval path, and unresolved impacts.

Next Step

Use the assessment to choose the next capability.

Use this pattern carefully

This example is illustrative. Adapt the workflow to the people, evidence, controls, and consequence level in your own context before using it.
