What are Learn, Apply, and Augment?

Learn builds understanding with free educational assets. Apply uses paid tools and playbooks with real workflows. Augment adds guided support for people, teams, and organizations.

Next step: Start with Learn, then move to Apply or Augment when your needs and readiness call for deeper support.
