Maturity → Integrating

Integrating: AI is part of the normal workflow.

Grounding, validation, ownership, and handoffs are becoming routine. The focus is now operational reliability: reducing reinvention while keeping responsibility and evidence visible.

How Integrating appears at each level

Individual

The person can ground output, verify it, and own the result as part of normal professional work.

Team

AI is part of the shared workflow, with increasingly stable handoffs, validation, backup coverage, and ownership.

Organization

Workflow, governance, and measurement are becoming connected instead of operating as separate initiatives.

Normal use is not the same as reliable use. Check whether controls survive exceptions, turnover, and higher consequence work.

Evidence to look for

Problem framing

Framing the task, desired outcome, and constraints is a normal part of the workflow.

Grounding and evidence

People use approved or relevant sources and make assumptions and limits visible.

Validation and judgment

Review thresholds are explicit and increase when the consequence of error increases.

Workflow integration

The AI-assisted sequence is part of normal operations and does not depend on one champion.

Ownership and communication

Handoffs, accountability, disclosure, and escalation paths are clear.

Learning and improvement

Correction load, exceptions, and near misses are used to improve the shared practice.

Practical next move

  1. Map where the workflow still depends on individual memory or reinvention.
  2. Make the strongest pattern teachable through shared inputs, checks, and handoff artifacts.
  3. Monitor reliability, correction load, and exceptions without weakening human accountability.

Common failure mode

Assuming adoption means integration. A workflow can be popular and still lack grounding, review, ownership, or a safe response to failure.

Ready to move to Leading when...

Controls and evidence hold during normal variation, people can support one another, and the practice is stable enough to teach without hiding its limits. Continue to Leading, or take the assessment.
