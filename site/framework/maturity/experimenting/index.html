Maturity → Experimenting

Experimenting: useful patterns are beginning to repeat.

Promising prompts, checks, or workflow steps are becoming deliberate. Reliability still varies by context, person, or consequence, so the next task is to make the pattern testable and safer to reuse.

How Experimenting appears at each level

Individual

The person repeats useful patterns and uses prompts, checks, and workflow steps on purpose, while still learning the limits.

Team

The team has repeatable patterns in parts of the work, but quality still depends on who is involved and how the handoff occurs.

Organization

Some practices are repeatable, but adoption, controls, and reliability remain uneven across functions.

A successful example is not yet a reliable operating standard. Test the pattern across representative conditions.

Evidence to look for

Problem framing

Templates or checklists help translate recurring work into a clear task and outcome.

Grounding and evidence

Source boundaries, assumptions, and constraints are documented well enough to repeat the work.

Validation and judgment

Review is matched to consequence, with known failure modes and explicit human checks.

Workflow integration

The AI step fits a selected workflow rather than sitting beside the work as an optional trick.

Ownership and communication

Ownership, handoff context, disclosure, and escalation are visible to the people involved.

Learning and improvement

Corrections and near misses are captured so the pattern improves instead of merely being repeated.

Practical next move

  1. Document the pattern, inputs, checks, owner, and stop conditions.
  2. Test it across different examples, users, and consequence levels.
  3. Measure correction load and update the pattern where the evidence shows weakness.

Common failure mode

Treating one successful pilot as proof of reliability. Variation is the signal to investigate, not an inconvenience to hide.

Ready to move to Integrating when...

The pattern works across representative contexts, controls are documented, correction load is understood, and another person can use the workflow without reinventing it. Continue to Integrating, or take the assessment.
