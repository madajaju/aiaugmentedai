Privacy Notice

This notice explains how the AI-Augmented website handles information when you browse the site, use an assessment, subscribe to the newsletter, or contact the site operator.

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Who is responsible

The operator responsible for this website is [insert legal entity or individual name]. Contact details are provided in the Contact section below. Add the operator's legal name, mailing address, and privacy contact before publication.

2. Information you provide

Depending on how you use the site, you may provide:

  • an email address when subscribing to the newsletter;
  • an email address when requesting assessment results by email;
  • messages or information you include when contacting the site operator; and
  • information you choose to include in a form, feedback message, or other interaction.

Do not submit confidential, regulated, sensitive personal, health, student, client, trade-secret, or other protected information unless you are authorized to do so and the submission channel is approved for that information.

3. Assessment and browser storage

The assessment runs in your browser. The current implementation stores your selected lens, stage, maturity, and update time in browser local storage under aa-journey-state. It also sets an aa_journey_state cookie that can persist for up to one year so the site can remember your path.

Assessment answers and calculated results are not sent to the site operator by the assessment form in the current implementation. If you choose to email your results, the email address is sent to the external newsletter provider described below. Your browser may remove this locally stored information through its settings, and the site cannot retrieve it after removal.

4. Campaign and usage information

If a page URL contains campaign parameters such as utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content, or utm_term, the site may store those values in session storage for the current browsing session. The site code can create in-browser engagement events, such as link clicks or assessment starts, for a measurement system if one is configured by the deployment.

The current repository does not include a configured analytics vendor. The hosting provider may independently collect standard technical logs such as IP address, browser, device, requested URL, and time of request. Confirm the hosting provider, log retention period, and any analytics or consent tools before publication and update this notice if they change.

5. Newsletter and emailed results

If you subscribe to the newsletter or request emailed assessment results, the form sends your email address to the external email provider configured for the site. That provider may process the address, subscription status, delivery information, and unsubscribe status to operate the mailing list. Review and identify the provider's current privacy notice, processing location, retention practices, and contractual terms before publication.

You can unsubscribe using the link in a newsletter message. Unsubscribing stops future promotional messages but may not remove records that must be retained for legal, security, or suppression-list purposes.

6. How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • operate, secure, maintain, and improve the website;
  • remember a journey or preference in your browser;
  • send newsletter messages or assessment results you requested;
  • respond to questions, requests, or support messages;
  • understand aggregate engagement and improve content; and
  • comply with legal obligations, protect rights, and prevent misuse.

The site operator will not use assessment answers to make decisions about your eligibility for employment, credit, insurance, health care, education, housing, or other services.

7. Legal bases and choices

Where privacy law requires a legal basis, the operator may rely on consent for optional newsletter communications, performance of a requested service for emailed results, legitimate interests or equivalent business purposes for site security and basic operations, and legal obligations where applicable. The final legal basis depends on the operator's location, audience, services, and deployment.

You may choose not to provide optional information. You can clear cookies and browser storage through your browser settings, and you can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time.

8. Sharing and service providers

The operator may share information with service providers that host the website, process forms, deliver email, provide security, or support site operations. Those providers may process information only as needed for their services and under their own terms or contractual obligations.

The operator may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to lawful requests, to protect users or the site, or in connection with a business transfer such as a merger, financing, acquisition, or sale of assets.

The operator does not sell personal information for money. Confirm whether the deployment uses advertising, cross-context behavioral advertising, data sharing, or any service that would change this statement.

9. Retention

Browser journey data remains until it expires or you clear it. Session campaign data normally remains only for the browser session. Newsletter and emailed-result information is retained by the email provider according to its configuration and legal obligations. Contact messages and technical logs are retained only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose collected, security, dispute resolution, legal compliance, or legitimate business records.

Before publication, the operator should set and document specific retention periods for each system and provider.

10. Security

The operator uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures appropriate to the information and the risk. No website, transmission, or storage system is completely secure. Do not send information through this site that you are not authorized to disclose or that requires controls the site has not confirmed.

11. Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and the operator does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the site. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact the operator so it can be reviewed and deleted where required. If the site is intentionally directed to students or collects information from minors, this section and the site's collection practices require additional legal review.

12. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information; to object to or restrict certain processing; to withdraw consent; or to complain to a privacy regulator. To make a request, use the contact information below. The operator may need to verify your identity and may have lawful reasons to deny or limit a request.

13. International processing

The operator and its service providers may process information in countries different from the country where you live. Confirm the countries involved and the safeguards used for international transfers before publication.

14. Changes to this notice

The operator may update this notice when its practices, services, or legal obligations change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Material changes should be communicated in a reasonably visible manner where required.

15. Contact

Privacy questions and requests should be sent to [insert privacy contact name and email]. Add the operator's legal name, mailing address, and any applicable regulator or representative contact before publication.

Publication checklist: This notice requires owner and legal review. Confirm the legal entity, hosting provider and logs, newsletter provider, form processing, analytics and consent tools, retention periods, international transfers, children and student audiences, jurisdiction-specific rights, and the correct privacy contact.
