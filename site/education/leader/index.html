What is changing

AI changes policy, support, assessment expectations, privacy decisions, and consistency across programs. AI can help with preparation, comparison, drafting, and pattern finding while responsibility remains with people.

Three capabilities that matter

Policy with purpose

Define what the institution is trying to protect or improve, then set expectations that match learning goals, privacy, safety, and accountability.

Institutional consistency

Make guidance, review thresholds, approved tools, and escalation paths understandable across programs without erasing local expertise.

Capability and support

Give faculty, staff, and students time, examples, training, and feedback so responsible practice can become part of normal work.
