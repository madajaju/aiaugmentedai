Apply: use AI to strengthen learning

Turn AI assistance into a visible study, research, or revision routine without outsourcing understanding or authorship.

Assess your starting point

Choose a learning workflow

Research

Use AI to clarify a question, compare perspectives, and identify sources. Verify claims against course material or primary evidence.

Revision

Ask for questions, counterarguments, or structure suggestions. Revise in your own words and retain the reasoning behind the changes.

Practice

Use AI as a tutor or sparring partner, then solve a new problem without the original response in front of you.

Run the AAOS method on your work

  1. Diagnose: define what you need to understand, produce, or practice.
  2. Activate: choose the AI role, prompt, source boundary, and learning constraint.
  3. Control: check accuracy, bias, citation, privacy, and whether the work still measures your understanding.
  4. Execute: draft, ground, validate, revise, and record the useful result.
  5. Measure and scale: compare your correction load and transfer to a new task before reusing the routine.

For a consequential submission or handoff, create a lightweight Learning Integrity Packet: outcome, assumptions, evidence, validation status, ownership, and risk.

What good looks like

  • You can explain the idea without repeating the AI response.
  • Your sources, assumptions, and AI contribution are visible where required.
  • You can identify what AI got wrong or left unresolved.
  • You can transfer the learning to a new problem, discussion, or assignment.

Avoid this

Do not mistake polished output or tool fluency for mastery. If AI removes every productive struggle, redesign the interaction so your understanding remains observable.
