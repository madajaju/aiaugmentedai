AI-Augmented Education

AI is changing how people learn, teach, assess, and lead. AI-Augmented Education helps people use that capability while protecting learning, judgment, privacy, and responsibility.

Find your starting pointUnderstand the framework

What does AI-Augmented mean in education?

It means using AI to strengthen learning and educational practice without outsourcing the thinking that education is meant to develop. Learners and educators define the purpose, examine sources, interpret context, and remain responsible for the work.

AI can support explanation, revision, lesson planning, feedback, and policy analysis. The right use depends on the learning goal, the learner or community, and the evidence needed to show understanding.

Where education work changes

Begin with a real learning or teaching workflow and make the purpose clear.

  • Learning: explore questions, compare explanations, revise ideas, and demonstrate transfer.
  • Teaching: plan lessons, provide feedback, design assignments, and protect assessment integrity.
  • Leadership: align policy, support, privacy, and institutional practice.

Use AI where it adds useful support. Keep the learning objective, human interpretation, and responsibility visible.
