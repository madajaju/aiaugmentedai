Learn: Use AI to improve teaching practice

Build a working understanding of learning objectives, lesson design, feedback quality, assessment integrity, then connect those ideas to the teachers work you actually do.

Explore the framework

Foundations for teachers

Learning objectives

State the learning goal, learner context, success criteria, and evidence of understanding before using AI to explore lesson options.

Lesson design

Connect activities, sequence, differentiation, and materials to the learning goal rather than accepting a generic lesson draft.

Feedback quality

Review AI-assisted feedback for accuracy, tone, accessibility, bias, and the learner action it should support.

Assessment integrity

Set clear AI expectations and verify that the assessment measures the intended learning and preserves student authorship.

Learn through a real workflow

Choose one recurring task, describe what good work requires, and discuss where AI may assist. Keep the learning objective, professional responsibility, and review points explicit.

Teacher learning loop

Diagnose the learning goal and learner context, Activate bounded planning assistance, establish Controls for source quality and student information, Execute with teacher review, Measure learning evidence, and Scale only what improves learning.

Practice output

Create a lesson or feedback record showing the objective, learner needs, AI contribution, teacher changes, evidence of learning, and next adjustment.

Human checkpoint

The teacher decides whether the plan is accurate, accessible, feasible, and aligned to the intended learning.
