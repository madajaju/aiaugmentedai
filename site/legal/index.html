AI-Augmented Legal Work

AI is changing how legal information is researched, drafted, reviewed, coordinated, and delivered. AI-Augmented Legal Work helps professionals use that capability while protecting judgment, confidentiality, accountability, and client trust.

Find your starting pointUnderstand the framework

What does AI-Augmented mean in legal work?

It means using AI inside a disciplined professional workflow. Legal professionals define the issue, provide authoritative context, verify authorities and facts, assess risk, and remain responsible for advice and work product.

AI can support research organization, drafting, comparison, issue spotting, and matter coordination. Fluency is not accuracy: consequential work requires review that matches the risk.

Where legal work changes

Start with a workflow where better context and explicit review can improve quality and trust.

  • Professional practice: organize authorities, prepare drafts, and surface questions for review.
  • Legal teams: preserve matter context, improve handoffs, and clarify ownership.
  • Firms: connect AI use to confidentiality, governance, quality standards, and operating models.

Use a consequence-matched validation approach and keep the source trail visible before work is delivered.

The framework helps teams diagnose the work, activate capability, establish controls, execute with review, measure reliability, and scale only when control stability supports it.
