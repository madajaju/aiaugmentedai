Augment: redesign legal practice around accountable AI assistance

Get guided support when a professional workflow needs stronger source control, review design, matter boundaries, or a repeatable path from draft to defensible advice.

What guided work can address

Research and authority review

Design a source-grounded workflow that separates discovery from authority verification and makes date, jurisdiction, and citation limits visible.

Client work product

Set review gates for drafts, advice, filings, and communications so professional judgment is explicit before release.

Personal practice system

Reduce correction load with reusable prompts, checklists, evidence capture, and an Individual Integrity Packet pattern.

Engagement outputs

  1. Workflow map showing where AI assists and where the professional must decide.
  2. Approved-use and confidentiality boundary for matter information.
  3. Consequence-matched validation ladder with escalation and stop conditions.
  4. Reusable Legal Work Integrity Packet and review checklist.
  5. Practice example tested against representative matters before reuse.

Who signs off

The responsible legal professional remains accountable. A qualified validator confirms authorities, facts, and professional fit; an approver accepts residual risk for medium- and high-consequence work.

Bring to the conversation

Bring one matter workflow, representative inputs and outputs, current review steps, correction examples, tool constraints, and the decision you want to make about adoption. Do not bring confidential client information unless the approved channel permits it.
