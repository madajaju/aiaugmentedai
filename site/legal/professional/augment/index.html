Augment: Legal Professional

Get guided support when legal professionals need help designing, reviewing, or governing a more complex AI-supported workflow.

When guided support helps

The workflow carries consequence

The work affects clients, patients, professional decisions, or institutional trust.

Responsibilities cross boundaries

Several people, roles, systems, or approval steps must work together.

The practice must be explainable

You need a clear record of context, controls, review, ownership, and evidence.

Prepare for the conversation

Bring the workflow, the people affected, known constraints, and the questions that remain open. Verify authorities and facts, protect confidential information, and keep the responsible professional in control of advice and work product.
