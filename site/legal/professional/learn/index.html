Learn: Legal Professional

Build a working understanding of research, drafting, authority checking, and matter-specific judgment in the context of legal professionals work.

Foundations for legal professionals

Context

Define the matter, client objective, authorities, confidentiality constraints, and consequence before using AI.

Review

Verify authorities, facts, citations, quotations, and procedural details at the level required by the work.

Accountability

Verify authorities and facts, protect confidential information, and keep the responsible professional in control of advice and work product.

Learn through a bounded workflow
