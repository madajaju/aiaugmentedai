Augment: make the legal team a reliable AI workflow

Get guided support to improve matter intake, handoffs, review queues, decision briefs, and shared standards without weakening confidentiality or professional accountability.

What guided work can address

Matter handoffs

Design a transfer pattern that carries client objective, source set, assumptions, open questions, validation status, and next ownership.

Review operations

Set consequence-matched queues, validator roles, return reasons, and escalation paths so review is judgment rather than approval theater.

Team capability

Turn individual experiments into a shared, teachable practice with examples, approved tools, and correction learning.

Engagement outputs

  1. Matter workflow and handoff map with confidentiality boundaries.
  2. Team Integrity Packet template for outcome, evidence, validation, ownership, and risk.
  3. Review threshold matrix for internal, client-facing, and external work.
  4. Role and escalation map for Prepared By, Validated By, Approved By, and manager intervention.
  5. Pilot report showing handoff return rate, correction load, and unresolved exceptions.

Team signoff

The work does not move when validation ownership is missing. The accountable professional or approver accepts residual risk only after the evidence and unresolved uncertainty are visible.

Bring to the conversation

Bring a representative handoff, review queue, matter taxonomy, tool list, access rules, examples of rework, and the boundary where the team needs a decision. Redact client information unless the approved channel permits its use.
