What does the assessment tell me?

It gives you a context-aware starting point. Your role and domain shape the questions, while AI-Augmented Maturity describes the condition of your current practice.

The result is meant to guide action, not label your ability. It helps you identify a workflow, capability, or control that is ready for deliberate improvement.

How it works

  1. Choose your context. Select the domain where the work happens.
  2. Choose your role. Select the responsibility closest to the workflow you want to improve.
  3. Review your next move. Use the maturity model and AAOS to choose a practical action.

Start with one real workflow. A focused assessment is more useful than a general opinion about AI.

Choose a domain

Context makes the assessment useful because the consequences, evidence, and responsibilities differ by field.

Choose a role

Begin with the role that owns or performs the work you want to examine.

  • Business Individual — personal research, drafting, and judgment.
  • Business Team — shared context, handoffs, and decisions.
  • Business Organization — governance, operating models, and scale.
  • Student — inquiry, revision, explanation, and learning integrity.
  • Teacher — lesson design, feedback, and assessment.
  • Education Leader — policy, support, privacy, and consistency.

What to do with the result

Use the result to choose a bounded workflow and a level of support. Learn when you need concepts and examples. Apply when you are ready to use tools and practice. Augment when the work needs guided facilitation, implementation, governance, or operating design.
