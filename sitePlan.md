## Authority and working rules

1. Use the canonical corpus for framework definitions, controlled vocabulary, AAOS stages, maturity levels, and named models.
2. Use current `site2026q2` pages for audience language, examples, assets, CTAs, and event material after checking claims.
3. Preserve the Domain → Role → Support hierarchy. Maturity and AAOS remain conceptual or framework dimensions.
4. Keep one primary next action per page. Use Learn for explanation, Apply for practical tools, and Augment for guided support.
5. Do not copy unsupported proof, testimonials, counts, institution names, or customer outcomes.
6. Legal and Medical sources are currently thin; keep those pages conservative until authoritative content is approved.

## Content gaps and decisions

- **Legal and Medical:** current sources provide routing placeholders but no complete domain evidence base. Keep the landing pages general and schedule authoritative role content before making stronger claims.
- **Education event content:** the current site contains IUC 2026 material. Reconcile event messaging with the permanent Education landing page after the event.
- **Practice:** current site pages do not provide direct equivalents for the new practice routes. Use grounded illustrative workflows from the role material and corpus stages; do not create customer case studies.
- **Support assets:** current global Apply and Augment pages are useful source material, but final content must remain specific to role, domain, and support level.
- **Framework terminology:** retain `Activate` for the public Stage 2 route, `Controls` for the approved stage label, `Validation Ladder` as the preferred term, and the distinction between AAOS sequence and maturity condition.
- **Proof:** migrate only claims that can be verified in repository sources. Otherwise qualify or remove them.
- **Legal readiness:** Privacy and Terms require legal review before launch.
